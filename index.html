Grilled Lime Coconut Chicken with Coconut Rice

Saved from an email; originally from melskitchencafe.com

Ingredients - Chicken and Marinade/Sauce

  • 3 tablespoons oil
  • Zest and juice of 1 lime
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons ground coriander
  • 2 tablespoons low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 1/2 cup canned coconut milk, light or regular
  • Pinch cayenne pepper
  • 2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breasts
  • 1/4 cup chopped fresh cilantro
  • Fresh limes, cut into wedges

Ingredients - Coconut Rice

  • 1 cup jasmine rice
  • 1 cup canned coconut milk, light or regular
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Directions

  1. Whisk together the oil, lime zest, lime juice, cumin, coriander, soy sauce, salt, sugar, curry powder, coconut milk, and cayenne pepper. Place the chicken in a large ziploc bag, pour the marinade over, seal, and refrigerate at least 2 hours and up to 8 hours.
  2. For the coconut rice, bring the rice, coconut milk, water and salt to a simmer in a medium saucepan. Cover, reduce the heat to low and simmer 15-16 minutes until the liquid is mostly evaporated. Remove from the heat and let stand 10 minutes before fluffing with a fork.
  3. Preheat a charcoal or gas grill to medium-high. Grill the chicken 6-7 minutes per side, depending on thickness. While the chicken cooks, pour the marinade into a saucepan, bring to a rolling boil and boil 10 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Plate the chicken, drizzle with sauce (reserve the rest to serve separately), sprinkle with fresh cilantro, and serve with lime wedges and extra sauce.

Beef Stroganoff

Saved from an email

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 clove garlic
  • 1 cup sliced mushrooms (or one small can)
  • 1 chopped onion
  • 1 can cream of mushroom soup
  • 1/2 container sour cream
  • Salt & pepper

Directions

  1. Saute the beef, garlic, onions and mushrooms together; salt and pepper to taste. Add the soup and simmer. Add the sour cream. Serve over egg noodles cooked in chicken broth - also great over baked potatoes.

Bruschetta Chicken Bake

Saved from an email

Ingredients

  • 1 can diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 1 pkg stuffing mix (like Stove Top)
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 1/2 lb chicken, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella

Directions

  1. Heat oven to 400 F. Mix the tomatoes, dry stuffing mix, water and garlic just until the stuffing mix is moistened.
  2. Layer the chicken, basil, cheese and stuffing in a 9x13 baking dish.
  3. Bake 30 minutes or until the chicken is cooked through.

Italian Sausage Sheet Pan Dinner

Saved from an email

Ingredients

  • 1 16-ounce package fully cooked Italian chicken sausages
  • 1 small red onion, peeled, halved, and sliced
  • 1 medium zucchini or yellow squash, halved and sliced
  • 1 large handful baby peppers, tops removed and halved
  • 1 large handful baby portobella mushrooms, quartered
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon Italian seasoning
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • Pinch red pepper flakes (optional)
  • Freshly grated parmesan cheese for serving

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 425 F. Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil.
  2. Cut each sausage into 4 pieces. Place on the baking sheet. Add the sliced onion, zucchini, peppers, and mushrooms and toss to evenly distribute.
  3. In a small bowl, whisk together the Italian seasoning, garlic, oil, salt, and red pepper flakes. Drizzle over the veggies and toss to combine.
  4. Bake 20 minutes, stirring halfway through. Sprinkle with freshly grated parmesan and serve immediately.

Korean Ground Beef and Rice Bowls

Saved from an email

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef (90% lean)
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 2 teaspoons sesame oil
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 teaspoon pepper
  • 2 cups hot cooked white or brown rice
  • Sliced green onions and sesame seeds for garnish

Directions

  1. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef and garlic over medium heat, breaking it into crumbles, until no longer pink.
  2. In a small bowl whisk the brown sugar, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger, red pepper flakes and pepper. Pour over the beef and simmer another minute or two.
  3. Serve over hot rice and garnish with green onions and sesame seeds.

Italian Beef

Saved from an email

Ingredients

  • 4-5 lbs boneless beef chuck roast (butcher twine removed)
  • 2 cups water
  • 2/3 ounce Italian seasoning mix (1 packet Good Seasons)
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 2 teaspoons black pepper
  • 2 teaspoons oregano
  • 2 teaspoons basil
  • 2 teaspoons onion powder
  • 2 teaspoons dried parsley
  • 2 teaspoons garlic powder
  • 2 teaspoons red pepper flakes (or to taste)
  • 2 bay leaves

Directions

  1. Mix all ingredients (except the roast) in a large crockpot. Place the roast in and cook on low 8-10 hours, until fork tender.
  2. Remove the beef and shred with two forks. Add back to the juices and enjoy.
  3. For traditional Chicago-style, pile high on a crusty roll with provolone slices and chopped giardiniera.

Creamy Taco Soup

Saved from an email

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 (10 fl oz) cans Ro-tel diced tomatoes & green chilies, with juices
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 (12 fl oz) can corn, drained
  • 1 (15 fl oz) can black beans, drained & rinsed
  • 1 (8 oz) block cream cheese
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Optional toppings: grated cheddar, avocado, cilantro, tortilla strips

Directions

  1. Take the cream cheese out of the fridge an hour ahead to soften, or microwave it 20-30 seconds.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a large soup pot over medium-high heat; spoon out excess fat.
  3. Add everything except the cream cheese and salt & pepper. Bring to a boil, then turn down to a gentle simmer for 8 minutes. Prep toppings while it cooks.
  4. Cut the cream cheese into smaller pieces and add to the soup, stirring until fully melted in. Season with salt & pepper and serve with toppings.

Notes

  • Beef broth works instead of chicken broth (darker color). Use low-salt broth if watching salt.
  • For a spicy version add 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper.
  • If not using Ro-tel, use regular diced tomatoes plus a can or two of diced green chilies (4 oz size).

How to make it
