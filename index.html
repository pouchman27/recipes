Apple Almond Quinoa with Maple Sausage

Recipe from iowagirleats.com

Source: iowagirleats.com

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup quinoa, pre-rinsed
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 3 oz maple breakfast sausage
  • 1 green onion, chopped
  • 1 small apple, chopped
  • 1/4 cup almonds, sliced or chopped
  • 2 tablespoons dried cranberries

Directions

  1. Bring chicken broth to a boil in a small saucepan, add the quinoa, cover, and simmer until the liquid is absorbed and the quinoa is tender, 12-15 minutes. Fluff with a fork, then transfer to a large mixing bowl to cool slightly.
  2. Meanwhile, heat a small skillet over medium heat. Add the sausage, brown it, and drain if necessary. Add the cooked sausage, green onions, apple, almonds, and cranberries to the slightly cooled quinoa and toss to combine. Serve warm or chilled.

Coconut Almond Breakfast Quinoa

Recipe from ourbestbites.com

Source: ourbestbites.com

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup quinoa, rinsed well and drained
  • 1 cup light coconut milk from a carton, such as Silk brand
  • 2 teaspoons brown sugar
  • 1/4 teaspoon almond extract

Ingredients - For Serving

  • Fresh berries
  • Toasted coconut
  • Additional coconut milk
  • Honey or agave syrup
  • Additional brown sugar if desired

Directions

  1. Place the rinsed quinoa, coconut milk, brown sugar, and almond extract in a medium pot. Stir and bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer and cover. Stir a few times during cooking; crack the lid if it threatens to boil over. Cook 10-15 minutes until the liquid is absorbed, adding a splash more water or coconut milk near the end if you like it softer and more porridge-like.
  2. Divide into bowls and top with fresh berries, toasted coconut, and a splash of coconut milk. Add a drizzle of honey or agave or a sprinkle of brown sugar if desired. Makes about 4 half-cup servings.

Crepes with Whipped Cream

From Kimberly

Ingredients

  • Crepes (4 servings): 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp butter, melted
  • Fillings: ham, cheese and fruit/berries (bananas, strawberries)
  • Whipped cream: 1 cup heavy cream, 4 tbsp powdered sugar, 1 tsp vanilla

Directions

  1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the flour and the eggs. Gradually add in the milk and water, stirring to combine. Add the salt and butter; beat until smooth.
  2. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium high heat. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1/4 cup for each crepe. Tilt the pan with a circular motion so that the batter coats the surface evenly.
  3. Cook the crepe for about 2 minutes, until the bottom is light brown. Loosen with a spatula, turn and cook the other side. Serve hot.
  4. Fill crepes with ham and cheese and fruit.
  5. To make whipped cream, beat the cream, vanilla and powdered sugar together for 10 minutes.

Crockpot Blueberry Breakfast Dish

From Marva

Ingredients

  • 2 cups blueberries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 tablespoon flour
  • 4 eggs
  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup maple syrup
  • 3 teaspoons cinnamon
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla
  • Cooking spray
  • 8 cups French bread, cut into cubes

Directions

  1. Pour the blueberries into a bowl and sprinkle with the tablespoon of flour. Stir gently, so you don't smush the berries. Set aside.
  2. In a large bowl, mix the eggs, milk, brown sugar, maple syrup, cinnamon, and vanilla until smooth.
  3. Spray the inside of your crock pot with cooking spray. Lay half the cubed French bread (4 cups) in the crock pot. Sprinkle half the blueberries (1 cup) over the bread randomly. Put in the rest of the bread, topped with the rest of the blueberries.
  4. Pour the egg and milk mixture over the top, and press down on the bread a little so it all gets wet, but not too squished.
  5. Cook on low for 3-4 hours.
  6. Serve warm, topped with whipped cream or ice cream.

Fluffy Pancakes

Recipe from allrecipes.com

Source: allrecipes.com

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons white vinegar
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 egg
  • 2 tablespoons butter, melted
  • Cooking spray

Directions

  1. Combine milk and vinegar in a medium bowl and set aside for 5 minutes to make sour milk.
  2. Combine flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large mixing bowl. Whisk egg and butter into the soured milk.
  3. Pour the flour mixture into the milk mixture and whisk until lumps are gone.
  4. Heat a large skillet over medium heat and coat with cooking spray. Pour 1/4 cupfuls of batter onto the skillet and cook until bubbles form and the edges are dry, 2-3 minutes. Flip and cook until browned on the other side. Repeat with remaining batter.

From Celene

Ingredients

  • 4 cups All-Bran (non-flake)
  • 1 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 quart buttermilk
  • 5 cups flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 5 teaspoons baking soda
  • 2 cups bran flakes (Raisin Bran, if you like raisins)

Directions

  1. Pour 2 cups boiling water on 4 cups of All-Bran (non-flake).

  • 1 1/3 cups flour
  • 4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 eggs, separated
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 1 3/4 cups milk

Directions

  1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together all dry ingredients.
  2. Separate the eggs, adding the yolks to the dry mixture and placing the whites in a small mixing bowl.

Boilermaker Tailgate Chili

From Abby

Source: allrecipes.com

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds ground beef chuck
  • 1 pound bulk Italian sausage
  • 3 (15 ounce) cans chili beans, drained
  • 1 (15 ounce) can chili beans in spicy sauce
  • 2 (28 ounce) cans diced tomatoes with juice
  • 1 (6 ounce) can tomato paste
  • 3 stalks celery, chopped
  • 1 large yellow onion, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, seeded and chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, seeded and chopped
  • 2 green chile peppers, seeded and chopped
  • 1 tablespoon bacon bits
  • 4 cubes beef bouillon
  • 1/2 cup beer
  • 1/4 cup chili powder, or more to taste
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tablespoon dried oregano
  • 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  • 2 teaspoons hot pepper sauce (such as Tabasco)
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • 1 teaspoon white sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt, or more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper, or more to taste
  • 1 (10.5 ounce) bag corn chips (such as Fritos)
  • 1 (8 ounce) package shredded Cheddar cheese

Directions

  1. Heat a large stock pot over medium-high heat. Crumble the ground chuck and sausage into the hot pan. Cook, stirring frequently, until evenly browned. Drain off excess grease.

Add the celery, onion, bell peppers, chile peppers, bacon bits, bouillon, beer, chili powder, garlic, Worcestershire sauce, oregano, cumin, hot pepper sauce, basil, cayenne, paprika, sugar, salt, and black pepper.   4. To serve, ladle hot chili into bowls and top with corn chips and Cheddar cheese.

Creamy Corn Chowder

Recipe from ourbestbites.com

Source: ourbestbites.com

Ingredients

  • 4 tablespoons butter, divided
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 1/2 cups milk
  • 2 teaspoons chicken base (plus extra broth if desired - see instructions)
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt, more to taste
  • 8 ounces bacon
  • 1 cup minced onion
  • 2 small-medium potatoes, cut into bite-sized pieces (about 2 cups)
  • 1 1/2 cups frozen corn
  • Black pepper
  • A few dashes of hot sauce, like Tabasco
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika (optional)
  • Grated cheddar cheese and green onions for serving (optional)

Directions

  1. Fry bacon until crisp (or lay flat on a foil-lined baking sheet and bake at 400 for about 20 minutes).
  2. Meanwhile, melt 2 tablespoons butter over low heat in a soup pot. Add the onion and saute 5-7 minutes until tender. Add remaining 2 tablespoons butter and the garlic; stir until butter is melted.
  3. Add flour and stir to coat the onions. Add the water very slowly while whisking until completely combined with no lumps.
  4. Add milk, chicken base, and 1/2 teaspoon salt (and smoked paprika if using); bring to a low simmer, not a rolling boil. Add potatoes and simmer, stirring very frequently, about 20 minutes until tender. (It will be very thick at this point; if you like it thinner, add up to 3 cups additional chicken broth.)
  5. Add crumbled bacon and corn and heat through. Add a few drops of hot sauce, black pepper, and additional salt to taste.
  6. Ladle into bowls and top with extra bacon, cheddar cheese, and green onions if you like.

Creamy Taco Soup

Saved from an email

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef
  • 1/2 red bell pepper, chopped
  • 2 (10 fl oz) cans Ro-tel diced tomatoes & green chilies, with juices
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1 (12 fl oz) can corn, drained
  • 1 (15 fl oz) can black beans, drained & rinsed
  • 1 (8 oz) block cream cheese
  • Salt & pepper to taste
  • Optional toppings: grated cheddar, avocado, cilantro, tortilla strips

Directions

  1. Take the cream cheese out of the fridge an hour ahead to soften, or microwave it 20-30 seconds.
  2. Brown the ground beef in a large soup pot over medium-high heat; spoon out excess fat.
  3. Add everything except the cream cheese and salt & pepper. Bring to a boil, then turn down to a gentle simmer for 8 minutes. Prep toppings while it cooks.
  4. Cut the cream cheese into smaller pieces and add to the soup, stirring until fully melted in. Season with salt & pepper and serve with toppings.

Notes

  • Beef broth works instead of chicken broth (darker color). Use low-salt broth if watching salt.
  • For a spicy version add 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper.
  • If not using Ro-tel, use regular diced tomatoes plus a can or two of diced green chilies (4 oz size).
